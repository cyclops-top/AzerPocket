Small by design

For a few friends and their Macs.

AzerPocket is a personal, non-commercial project for an initial group of 2–4 users. There are no fees, advertisements, subscriptions, or donations. The desktop application is currently in early development; the features above describe the intended scope.

Respect the creators

Original authors stay in the picture.

Planned downloads will respect author permissions and retain author credits, original project links, and support links where available. AzerPocket is not a public addon mirror; backups are intended for locally installed addons.

CurseForge API access has not yet been approved. Any future integration is subject to approval, applicable terms, and each author’s third-party distribution settings.

AzerPocket is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment, Overwolf, or CurseForge. World of Warcraft belongs to Blizzard Entertainment. Third-party addons retain their respective licenses.
